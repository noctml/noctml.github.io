- Evaluation: the page covers Bonn RGB-D Dynamic, Wild-SLAM MoCap, DAVIS-16/17 MOS, Sintel tracking, qualitative reconstruction, video depth, and ablation.
- Conclusion / limitation: the page preserves the paper's claim that feed-forward dense priors and online SLAM optimization can be mutually helpful, with runtime still limited by multi-frame inference.
